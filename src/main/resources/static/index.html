    How it works

    1. 1
      Grow your savings

      1 point for every euro above your highest savings total ever.

    2. 2
      Add €50 of new savings in a week

      That secures the week. Each week in a row adds 10% bonus points, up to 1.50×.

    3. 3
      Leave it untouched

      Every year a deposit stays put, it pays another 10% of its points. Take it out early and you miss that year.

    4. 4
      Trade in your points

      Coffee, a snack, cinema, a museum or a donation. Points stay valid for 12 months.
